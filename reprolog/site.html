Debug from the actual request

No more guessing from a stack trace. Everything around the failure is saved next to it.

Live activity

Requests stream in as they happen. Filter by status, path or duration and spot the slow ones.

Spans and logs together

Logfire and OpenTelemetry spans as a waterfall, with each log line attached where it was written.

Replay on your laptop

reprolog replay <id> pulls the log from your dashboard and re-runs the exact request, with HTTP and LLM calls served from the recording.

Outbound calls

httpx and requests calls, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and Groq, with bodies and token usage.

Private by default

Passwords, tokens, cookies and auth headers are redacted inside your app, before anything is sent. Free logs are deleted after 7 days, Pro after 30.

Projects & tokens

One write token per app, a project per environment: dev, staging, prod. Switch between them in the dashboard.

Add it to FastAPI in five steps

Sign up, create a write token, add two lines. Failed requests show up in your dashboard within seconds.
